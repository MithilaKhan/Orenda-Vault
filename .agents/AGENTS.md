# Orenda AI: Brand Personality & UI Text Guidelines

## Core Identity
Orenda AI is a premium AI-powered personal knowledge workspace designed to help users capture, organize, and rediscover their digital information. **It is NOT a conversational AI platform.** AI is a supporting feature that helps users organize and retrieve their own knowledge with minimal token usage.

**Target Audience:** Developers, designers, students, freelancers, and knowledge workers who want a calm, distraction-free workspace.

## Brand Personality
- Professional
- Calm
- Minimal
- Intelligent
- Premium
- Nature-inspired
- Trustworthy
- Productive
- Clean and modern

## Writing Style & Voice
Inspired by Apple, Notion, Linear, Arc Browser, and Raycast.
- **Short & Clear**: Avoid unnecessary adjectives, marketing buzzwords, or lengthy explanations.
- **Human & Professional**: Confident but friendly language.
- **Action-oriented & Helpful**: Focus on productivity and getting things done.

### DO USE
- Create a new note
- Search your knowledge
- Save for later
- Recently updated
- Nothing here yet
- Your content is organized automatically.
- AI generated a summary for this note.
- Find information instantly.

### AVOID (No Chatbot Phrases)
- Ask me anything
- I'm your AI assistant
- What can I help you with today?
- Start chatting
- Let's explore together
- Generate magic

## UI Text Guidelines
- **Buttons**: Create, Save, Search, Upload, Organize, Continue, Archive, Restore, Share
- **Main Navigation**: Dashboard, Notes, Collections, Documents, Recent Activity, Favorites, AI Search, Settings
- **Empty States**: "No notes yet. Create your first note to start building your knowledge library."
- **Success Messages**: "Note saved successfully.", "Collection created.", "Summary generated.", "Changes saved."
- **Error Messages**: "Unable to load your content.", "Something went wrong.", "Please try again."

## Ultimate Goal
Every piece of text should reinforce that Orenda AI is a premium knowledge management platform with lightweight AI assistance. The experience should feel focused, elegant, and productivity-driven.
